# Week-1 dummy-macro floorplan (Gate A condition 2)

**Goal.** Find out, before any real RTL exists, whether the 5-macro plan routes at 8x4 within GitHub's time limit and passes precheck.

**Configurations:**

| Name | Macros | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| `fp5` | 4 × RM_IHPSG13_1P_64x64 (784.48 × 64.36 µm) + 1 × RM_IHPSG13_2P_256x32 (702.83 × 136.97 µm) | two rows of two 64x64 along one long edge; the 2P macro along the opposite edge; pins facing inward |
| `fp3` (Plan B) | 2 × 64x64 + 1 × 2P 256x32 | same pattern |

**Filler.** Flop chains plus XOR clouds sized to about 0.44 mm² of standard cells, wired to every macro port so nothing is optimised away. Use `(* keep *)` or real connectivity.

**Record in `RESULTS.md`:**
- routing time;
- DRC, LVS and antenna counts;
- precheck result;
- setup and hold slack at the slow, typical and fast corners, each reported separately.

**Known traps:**
- The CMOS5L tile's PDN is Metal4, and the macro power columns must line up with the stripes. Ken Pettit's numbers are for 512x32 only; read the 64x64 and 2P 256x32 LEFs.
- Recipes: Loom `docs/tt_cmos5l_facts.md` §11, TD-µT `src/pdn_macro_bridge.tcl`, Ken Pettit's IHP SRAM walkthrough.
- 8x4 is supported by the official tools (docs/notes/TT_TEMPLATE.md, corrected 2026-10-20); kdp1965 `cmos-8x4` is not needed.

## Findings before the first harden (2026-10-20)

Measured from the macro LEFs at IHP-Open-PDK `2bbec755` (the revision `tt-gds-action@ihp-cmos5l` installs; CMOS5L's
`sg13cmos5l_sram` is a symlink to `sg13g2_sram`). Command: `python3 stripe_fit.py <macro>.lef` plus the LEF parse in the
step-7 session notes.

| Macro | Size (µm) | Metal4 power columns |
|---|---|---|
| every 1P macro (64x64, 256x32, 512x16, ...) | 1P_64x64: 784.48 × 64.36; 1P_256x32: 416.64 × 118.78 | VDD! / VDDARRAY! at 5.665 + 11.24 k, VSS! 5.62 µm to the right |
| every 2P macro (256x32, ...) | 2P_256x32: 702.83 × 136.97 | VDD at 8.355 + 17.68 k, VSS 8.84 µm to the right |

1. **The 5-macro plan as written (4 × 1P_64x64 + 1 × 2P_256x32) cannot share one block stripe grid.** The two families
   differ in column pitch (11.24 vs 17.68) and in the power-to-ground spacing (5.62 vs 8.84); Tiny Tapeout blocks have one
   Metal4 stripe layer with one pitch and one pair spacing, and the precheck requires every Metal4 power port to be
   full height (Loom `docs/tt_cmos5l_facts.md` §11), so stripes cannot simply stop at a macro.
2. **Wide 1P macros have a periphery band in the middle** whose columns are offset: for 1P_64x64 the right half's grid
   is shifted 8.83 µm (mod 11.24) against the left half, so no uniform pitch puts every stripe on a column of its own net
   (`stripe_fit.py` finds 0 fitting positions for 1P_64x64 and 1P_256x32 on Loom's 67.44 µm grid). Narrow macros
   (236.8 µm wide, Loom, TeslaCoilerOW) have no band.
3. **Known working recipe for wide 1P macros at 8x4:** Ken Pettit's PRISM (`kdp1965/ihp-um-janestreet-prism`, Apache-2.0)
   adds a LibreLane step (`odb_stripes.py`, `librelane_plugin_prism_pdn.py`) after GeneratePDN that draws full-height
   stripes on each SRAM's own power columns, with a 44.96 µm (4 × 11.24) block pitch.
4. **Proposed configurations, in order:**
   - `fp5`: 4 × 1P_64x64 (edge memory) + 1 × **1P**_256x32 (coverage), PRISM's stripe step. All five macros share the 11.24
     family. This changes S10 (dual-port coverage macro, FROZEN) only if adopted for the design: a single-port
     read-modify-write at one update per 2 cycles is enough, because R9.3 covers one engine at a time and an engine fires
     at most every E + 1 = 2 cycles. **Needs the owner's decision before the design changes.**
   - `fp5_2p`: the plan as written, with a custom stripe step that also handles the 17.68 family. Research risk.
   - `fp3` (Plan B): 2 × 1P_64x64 + coverage, same recipe.

## fp5 diagnostic run: where the Metal4 shorts are (2026-10-21)

CI 39531747585 (`fc4500d`): detailed routing capped at 12 iterations (`DRT_OPT_ITERS`), `ERROR_ON_TR_DRC` off, `runs/`
uploaded. Routing took 15 min (02:16:50 to 02:31:52) and left **245 violations, all on Metal4** (169 shorts, 76
spacing); Metal2 and Metal3 were clean. The flow then ran to LVS, where LibreLane crashed parsing netgen's output
(`JSONDecodeError: Invalid \escape`; probably the escaped `\[` net names, not investigated).

From `45-openroad-detailedrouting/tt_um_srujan0006_fp5.drc` [Measured, classification script over the bounding boxes]:
- **every violation lies over (225) or within 5 µm of (20) a 1P_64x64 macro**: u_m2 130, u_m3 80, u_m1 35; none over
  u_m0, the coverage macro or the open core;
- partners: signal vs VPWR/VGND stripe 158, signal vs the macro's own Metal4 shapes 87;
- 109 are signal wires running the full macro height on Metal4 (e.g. `u_m1.qr[0]` at x = 404.06, y 189.00–253.36).

Cause [Measured from the PDK LEF]: the 1P_64x64 LEF obstructs Metal1 and Metal3 over the whole macro but Metal4 only on
48 % of it (201 rectangles, the power columns); the gaps between columns look free to the router, which uses them as
vertical tracks across the macro and then cannot fit them between the macro's columns and our extended stripes.
PRISM met the same problem and fixed it with routing obstructions over the SRAM interiors on Metal2–Metal4, leaving
2.6 µm free at the pin face (`kdp1965/ihp-um-janestreet-prism` `docs/prism_interface.md`, sections 4b and 4d.1).
Signal pins of all three SRAM types are Metal2 on the y = 0 face (N; FS puts them on top) [Measured, LEF].

Fix applied: `gen_variants.obstructions()` writes `ROUTING_OBSTRUCTIONS` for every macro (fp5 and all variants).

Post-route STA of this run (20 ns clock, light filler, with the shorts still present, so indicative only):

| slow 1.08 V 125 °C | +1.291 ns (≈ +2.09 ns at 20.8 ns) | +0.009 ns (0) |

## fp5 with routing obstructions (CI 39535356190, 2026-10-21): better, still not routed

The obstructions worked as intended for the bulk of the routing: violations fell from 3,011 to 183 in the first 4 min
of detailed routing (the run without them: 30 → 32 stuck for hours). The first routing run then crawled to **54 after
64 passes** (02:55–05:20, single-threaded); antenna repair re-routed twice (92, then ~150) until the 320-min step
timeout. Global routing reported 633 overflowing tiles at 11 % total usage, i.e. the overflow is exactly where the
obstructions are: with `GRT_ALLOW_CONGESTION` the global router plans nets **through** the blocked macro area and the
detailed router follows those guides.

All 54 remaining violations are over u_m1, u_m2 or u_m3 (none over u_m0 or the coverage macro), Metal4 42 and Metal3
12: wires crossing a macro and shorting to the obstruction, a stripe or the macro's own shapes [Measured, DRC report
`drt-run-0`]. Example: `net312` (a fanout buffer of the shared filler input) has sinks at y ≈ 95 (channel above u_m0),
272 (the pin-less channel between u_m1 and u_m2) and 465–488 (channel above u_m2) [Measured, placed DEF]. The four
1P_64x64 macros are 784 µm walls starting at the left die edge, so the only legal path between channels is around
their right end, a detour of 600 µm or more for these nets.

Cause [Estimate]: the floorplan, not the macros. It has four full-width walls, a channel between u_m1 and u_m2 with no
pins in it, and walls closed on the left. Candidates for the next run: back-to-back pairs (N under FS) so there are
two walls instead of four and every channel faces pins; walls moved off the die edge so nets can pass at both ends;
`OPENROAD_THREADS` set to 4 (runs are single-threaded now: `openroad -threads None`, CPU time = wall time).

**Next fp5 run (2026-10-21):** the four 1P_64x64 macros as two back-to-back pairs (N below FS, 68.04 µm apart, so no
cell row fits between them) at x = 464.20 (on the 11.24 µm grid; walls 464.20–1248.68, open at both ends):
pair A at y 113.40 / 181.44, pair B at y 464.94 / 532.98. Channels: bottom 3.78–113.40 (u_m0 pins), middle

## CI speedups (2026-10-22)

1. **Fail fast:** detailed routing capped at 15 passes (`DRT_OPT_ITERS`; LibreLane's default is 64), the GDS step at
   120 min (job 150). Converging routes reached 0 within a few passes (fp5: pass 4); none that was stuck at pass 10
   converged later.
2. **No PNG render:** experiment workflows use `.github/actions/harden`, the steps of tt-gds-action@3412659 without
   the render (13 min 54 s of fp5's 27 min job), its apt packages, or the GDS_logs upload. `tt_submission` is kept, so
   the stock precheck still runs. `gds.yaml` keeps the stock action.
3. **No wasted runs:** every experiment workflow has a `concurrency` group that cancels a stale run of itself;
   `gds.yaml` is manual-only until the SPEX RTL exists.

Setup is not a bottleneck and is not cached further: apt and pip are already cached by the action, the PDK fetch takes
17 s and the LibreLane image pull 79 s (fp5 run 39575347791).
